### 🎯 **Centering**  
**Category**: Arcana  
**Attributes**: Discipline + Wit  
**Type**: Active Use Skill

#### 📘 Summary
Centering is the practiced act of aligning the mind and magical flow to reduce **Burn**—the spiritual strain of spellcasting. It’s a cornerstone for any dedicated spellcaster, providing flexible options both in and out of combat.

---

### 🔹 **Snap Focus**  
**Use**: *In combat*  
**AP Cost**: 10 – (⅓ × Centering Skill Ranks)  
**Effect**:  
- Roll Centering  
- Remove Burn equal to Degrees of Success (DoS)  
- Gain Fatigue equal to **½ Burn removed** (rounded down)  
**Limit**: Once per round
---

### 🔹 **Steady Focus**  
**Use**: *Out of combat*  
**Time**: 10 minutes of calm meditation  
**Effect**:  
- Roll Centering  
- Remove Burn equal to DoS  
**Requirements**: Peaceful, undisturbed setting
---
### 🔹 **Enduring Focus**  
**Use**: *Out of combat*  
**Time**: 1 hour of uninterrupted meditation  
**Effect**:  
- Roll Centering  
- Remove Burn equal to **3 × DoS**  
**Requirements**: Tranquil environment, no stressors
